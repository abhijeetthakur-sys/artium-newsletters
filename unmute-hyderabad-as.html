A playful, expressive take on a song that asks for innocence and confidence in the same breath.

  ✦  

Alyna walked onto the unMute stage in Hyderabad excited and a little nervous. Then she looked out at the room. “Seeing the audience smile and connect with the song gave me confidence,” she told us afterwards. “It made the performance special.”

In her words, “Ghodey Pe Sawaar” is a playful yet expressive song — one that blends innocence, confidence, and beautiful storytelling. The unMute version is a reimagined take on the original, and for Alyna that opened something up: the room to carry its emotions her own way.

Right before she started singing, she thought of her teacher, Debadrita Ma'am — her guidance, and one simple instruction Alyna gave herself: enjoy the performance.

One detail from rehearsal travelled with her onto the stage. The arrangement has long music gaps, and JD Sir added a few notes for her to sing through them. On the night, those notes kept her connected with the audience from the first line to the last.

When she walked off, she felt all the hours of practice had paid off. Happy, proud, and thankful for the chance.

A note from the Talent Department

Ask most young singers about a live set and they'll tell you about the big notes. Alyna told us about the gaps.

“Ghodey Pe Sawaar” has long music breaks — stretches where a singer can drift away from the room. In rehearsal, JD Sir added a few notes for her to carry through them. A small change. On stage in Hyderabad, it meant she never lost the audience, and she noticed it while it was happening.

That awareness is the real takeaway. Between Debadrita Ma'am's guidance in class and one well-placed rehearsal note, she walked off with more than a good performance: she walked off with the confidence to do it again. She tells us she wants to keep learning and explore different genres next. Hyderabad was a good place to start.

unMute does not wait for an artist to be famous before giving them a proper stage. That is the whole point.

Watch it. Share it if it moves you.

About artium unMute

unMute is Artium Academy's live performance series, built as a format, not a one-off event. It's a recurring show with its own sound, its own city editions, and a roster of artists who return season after season.

Each episode is shot and produced to a fixed format, city by city, so the show scales without losing what makes it work: real performances, minimal gloss, teachers and students sharing the same stage.

unMute is where Artium's talent gets a platform beyond the classroom, and where the audience gets to watch music get made, not just performed.
