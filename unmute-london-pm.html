An Asha Bhosle classic, slowed down for the unMute stage and sung live at CoLabs - OPEN, Southall.

  ✦  

Preksha Mathur brought an Asha Bhosle classic to London. Aage Bhi Jaane Na Tu, composed by Ravi with lyrics by Sahir Ludhianvi, sung live at CoLabs - OPEN in Southall on 25 July, at unMute London.

Asked what the song means to her, she kept it short: “to live in the present moment.” The unMute arrangement slowed it down, and for Preksha that changed how the song read. “The new track slowed down its pace and made it more distinctive to be able to understand its meaning.”

Walking on, she was “super excited to present it in front of live audience but nervous at the same time because of performance pressure.” Midway, the nerves caught up with her. “There was a point where my confidence was shaking a bit and I had to look at the lyrics again.”

One note from rehearsals with Jaydeep Bagwadkar stayed with her the whole way through: “The focus on pitching correctly on lower notes is important than singing higher notes.”

Walking off, she “felt I could have done it better and also a sigh of relief.” What’s changed since? “It has definitely improved my stage courage and made me understand that singing on studio mic is completely different than on the live stage mic.”

A note from the Talent Department

Preksha walked off stage feeling she could have done it better. That is an honest thing for any performer to say, and the right place to start from.

Her wobble came mid-song, when she had to glance back at the lyrics. She kept going. She held on to what Jaydeep told her in rehearsal, to get the lower notes right before worrying about the high ones, and she finished the song.

That is what a live stage teaches that a studio mic never will, and she said so herself. She came away with more stage courage. That is the thing we hope every unMute performer takes home.

unMute does not wait for an artist to be famous before giving them a proper stage. That is the whole point.

Watch it. Share it if it moves you.

Abhijeet Thakur

About artium unMute

unMute is Artium Academy's live performance series, built as a format, not a one-off event. It's a recurring show with its own sound, its own city editions, and a roster of artists who return season after season.

Each episode is shot and produced to a fixed format, city by city, so the show scales without losing what makes it work: real performances, minimal gloss, teachers and students sharing the same stage.

unMute is where Artium's talent gets a platform beyond the classroom, and where the audience gets to watch music get made, not just performed.
