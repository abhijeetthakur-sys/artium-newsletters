Not a voice over a backing track — three musicians on a carpet, listening to each other in real time.

  ✦  

Akshita Pola brought a Narayana Teertha composition to a stage that had spent the evening on film songs and reimagined covers. Govardhana Giridhara, set in Darbari Kaanada — a raga she chose because she likes its phrases, and because, as she puts it, "my Carnatic music is my strength."

She did not come alone. Koundinya Pannala sat to one side with a violin, Kolipaka Veeraswamy to the other with a mridangam, and the three of them took the carpet the way a Carnatic set is meant to be taken — nobody following a click, everybody listening.

Ask her which part of the composition she loves and she does not hesitate: shree vatsankita sri kausthuba dhare. Her favourite line in the whole piece.

In the seconds before she began, she was thinking about Shubha ma'am — the teacher who taught her this song, patiently, over a long time. She decided then and there to dedicate the performance to her. Her way of saying thank you.

Shubha ma'am had left her with one instruction, and it is the best piece of advice in this entire newsletter. Perform like a maestro, not like a student. Akshita walked off "too happy to perform my best."

A note from the Talent Department

Akshita could have picked something the room already knew. A film song, something that fit the evening. Instead she brought a Narayana Teertha composition in Darbari Kaanada, and a violinist and a mridangam player with her.

That takes a particular kind of confidence, and she is clear about where it comes from. "I feel my Carnatic music is my strength." She was not adapting herself to the stage. She trusted that the stage would come to her.

And then she gave the whole thing away — dedicated it to Shubha ma'am, who taught her the song. A student who is already thinking about who put her there is a student worth watching.

unMute does not wait for an artist to be famous before giving them a proper stage. That is the whole point.

Watch it. Share it if it moves you.
